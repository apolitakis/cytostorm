# Name brainstorm (2026-10-07)

**Decision (Alex, 2026-10-07): Cytostorm is the working title ("wins for now").**

Pitch the names have to fit: Plants vs Zombies but immune system. Glowing cells on black,
neutrophils with guns, chaotic waves, a Body output slider that builds fever and fatigue,
and a cytokine storm that can kill you. iPhone, shared with friends first.

Collision check: a quick web search only, not a trademark or App Store search.
