Cytostorm v3 Tuner

Pick a level, change its pressure or the rules, then let bots play it. The bots tab shows who wins and checks the standing rules. Winning strategies tries many different ways to play and shows which choices win.

Every knob is part of a level's script or a fixed rule. Nothing reacts to how the player is doing.
