Fluorescence on black. One hue per side.

Lightweight art for the fun-test prototype: glowing cells on a dark tissue field, the way a fluorescence microscope sees them. Friendly cells are cyan, bacteria are magenta, antibodies are gold, and the toxin is violet. Shape does the rest, so it still reads for colour-blind players.

The simpler v2 game

landscape phone · mock, not the game

Neutrophils zigzag and fire gold tracers. Inside the middle zone's Support ring they get speed trails and fire bigger, white-hot tuned shots that pierce the armored bacteria; plain shots spark off the armor. Every 12 seconds the Wound blinks red and a violet shockwave sweeps it. The Lymph node timer fills red, with a heartbeat while bacteria are inside, and the progress bar marks waves (magenta circles) and toxin bursts (violet diamonds).

Fatigue meter

ART.fatigueMeterSVG(fatigue, tiers) · sits under the Body output slider

Faint bands preview each tier, so the player can see what's next before reaching it. The fill takes the colour of the band it has reached, and a white edge marks where fatigue is now. When the player is Feverish the play area's edges glow orange and breathe; when Exhausted they turn red with a slow, tired heartbeat (ART.drawFatigueEdge).

Storm preview on the fatigue meter

ART.fatigueMeterSVG(f, tiers, 10, { ghost: 50, burn: 16 })

While the storm button is held, the meter shows where fatigue would land: a hatched bar for the instant 50-point cost, then a fading band for the afterburn's range, so the gamble is informed but not exact. White means you'd survive; the afterburn band turns red if it could reach 100, and the whole ghost turns red when the storm is certain to kill. During the afterburn the meter gets a red outline.

Using it in the prototype

prototype/assets/assets.js
// One file, no fetches: every SVG is a string in ART.svg, plus ART.palette and ART.meta.
await ART.loadAll();
// Pre-render once per on-screen size, then blit hundreds per frame.
const neu = await ART.sprite('neutrophil', 22);
ctx.globalCompositeOperation = 'lighter';   // glows add up like a real fluorescence image
ctx.drawImage(neu, x - 11, y - 11, 22, 22);
// Map sprites share a 64-unit box centred on the cell; ART.meta[name].radius is the body radius in those units.
